I am building an architecture for proving that real images and videos are truly real (not AI-generated). We do so by attaching metadata, such as time and GPS location, to images and videos. The metadata must be true, uneditable (i.e., tampering is detectable), and [verifiable](https://verify.contentauthenticity.org/) by anyone. My advisor is Prof. [Andrew Kwong](https://andrewkwong.org).

In 2023, I graduated with highest distinction from UNC with a B.S. in Mathematics and a B.S. in Computer Science.

At UNC, I was instructor of record for COMP 210 and co-taught COMP 311. I was also a TA for several COMP courses.

A fun fact about me: I used to [speedcube](/blog/2021/cubing).
